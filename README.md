# Alcohol Excise Tax Drawback

Recover federal and state excise tax on exported, transferred, returned, or destroyed alcohol products.

**Primary buyer:** Brewers, distillers, wineries, and exporters. **Evidence:** production records, taxpaid removals, formulas, transfers, exports, destructions, excise returns, proof of export, claims, and refunds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Producer permit registry
- Product formula registry
- Production lot tracking
- Taxpaid removal ingestion
- Bonded transfer control
- Export shipment matching
- Proof-of-export evidence
- Destruction loss evidence
- Rate and credit calculation
- Claim eligibility
- Statute deadline monitoring
- Drawback claim preparation
- Agency query workflow
- Refund reconciliation
- Product market analytics
